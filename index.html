Questions

How is this different from hiring a videographer?

A videographer just hits record. I bring over fifteen years of sales experience to the table. I don't just capture footage; I build a strategy to help you connect with clients and grow your business. We're not making disposable content. We're building trust assets.

Do I have to come up with ideas?

No. That's my job. During our strategy session, we'll uncover the questions your clients are already asking. I turn those into scripts. You just show up, read the teleprompter, and I coach you through it.

Most people feel that way at first. The studio is set up to make it easy: teleprompter, good lighting, and I coach you through every take until it feels natural. By your second or third session, you won't think twice about it.
