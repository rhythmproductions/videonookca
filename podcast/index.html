Questions

Do I need to have a podcast already?

No. Some people book a session just to find out whether they like doing it.

If you want the back end sorted, that's the launch setup, $297 one time. I set up your hosting, your distribution to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube, and get your cover art in place. After that you upload and hit publish.

How many people fit?

Two is the standard session. A third seat is $50 and comes with its own camera. Three chairs in the room, with up to three cameras.

When do I get my files?

Same day. You walk out with one horizontal 1080p video file, already cut live between the two angles as you talk, audio baked in. You also get the audio on its own track so you can publish to the podcast platforms.

Want every individual camera and audio file? That's $97. Want the whole thing in 4K with the raw files included? That's $147. Either one, 48 business hours.

I've never done this before. Is that a problem?

No. Most first-timers are nervous about the gear, and the gear is the part that's already handled.

Bring someone to talk to and a rough idea of what you want to cover. I'll get you set and your levels sorted before we roll.

Do you edit it?

Not in the session price. The base session hands you a finished file the live cut already made, and that's enough to publish.

If you want a real edit, multicam with mixed audio and titles, that's a produced episode. Starts at $997, and it includes a round of revisions.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Cameras, mics, lighting, and someone running it are all included. Bring the conversation.

What if I want to do this regularly?

Book weekly or monthly and you get a standing slot on the calendar with priority scheduling. Same time, held for you.

If you'd rather pay up front, the four-pack is $1,788 for four sessions, good for six months.

Where is the studio, and when can I book?

The studio's in Armstrong. Tons of parking, easy highway access.

Sessions run 9am to 5pm, Monday through Friday.
